= RFD 10 Power inventory and topology

== Goal

Model how power reaches equipment, from the panel to the power supply, so
operators can answer what loses power when a feed fails, which devices lack
redundant supply, and how close each feed is to its limit. RFD 5 left power
feeds, panels, outlets, and power paths to a later RFD; this is that RFD.

Power follows the same rule as the rest of Renga: planned connections, observed
evidence, and the current topology stay distinct, and evidence never rewires
the plan silently.

== NetBox reference

NetBox models power panels at a site or location, power feeds from a panel to a
rack, power outlets on PDUs, and power ports on devices, joined by cables. A
feed carries its supply type, phase, voltage, amperage, and maximum utilization;
a power port carries its maximum and allocated draw. Renga keeps that shape and
adds observed draw and supply health as evidence.

== Model

* `PowerPanel`: a named panel placed at a site or location from RFD 5.
* `PowerFeed`: one circuit from a panel, with supply (AC or DC), phase (single
  or three), voltage, amperage, maximum utilization (default 80%), and a
  redundancy side (`a`, `b`, or none). A feed may be assigned to a rack.
* Power outlets and power ports are typed endpoints created from the RFD 6
  catalog templates (`power_outlet` and `power_port`), the same way interfaces
  come from interface templates. A PDU is an ordinary `pdu` resource with outlet
  templates. A server's power supplies give it power ports.
* A power port carries its maximum draw and allocated draw in watts; an outlet
  carries its feed leg on three-phase PDUs.
* Power connections reuse RFD 7's plan, assertion, and current-cable model with
  a `power` cable kind. A connection joins a power port to an outlet, or a PDU's
  inlet port to a feed.

Every power object gets a resource envelope and typed projection, like the
other DCIM objects.

== Power path

The current power path of a device is the chain from each of its power ports
through an outlet, its PDU, and the PDU's inlet to a feed and panel. Renga
computes it from current power connections; it is never stored as a separate
truth. A port whose chain is incomplete shows where it stops.

Redundancy is a property of the path: a device is redundantly powered when its
ports reach at least two feeds on different sides. A device with two supplies
both on side `a` is not redundant, whatever its supply count says.

== Capacity and utilization

A feed's capacity is voltage × amperage × (√3 for three-phase) × maximum
utilization. Allocated load sums the allocated draw of every port on the feed's
path. Measured load comes from evidence when a source reports it.

Renga shows both, never one in place of the other: allocated load answers "can
I plug in another server", measured load answers "is this feed actually hot".
Three-phase PDUs show load per leg, because one overloaded leg trips while the
feed as a whole looks fine.

== Evidence

Power evidence arrives through observations like everything else:

* host agents report power supplies as components (RFD 6), with status and
  input watts when the platform exposes them;
* PDU and UPS collectors (RFD 11) report outlet state, per-outlet and per-leg
  current, and inlet voltage;
* BMC sources may report supply health and draw.

Evidence never creates or removes a power connection. An outlet reporting draw
with no connected port, or a supply reporting no input, opens a finding that an
operator resolves, as LLDP evidence does for data cables in RFD 7.

== Findings

* **Not redundant**: a device whose power ports do not reach two sides, when its
  hardware type has more than one power port.
* **Feed near capacity**: allocated or measured load above the feed's maximum
  utilization, or a three-phase leg out of balance.
* **Supply failed**: a power supply reported failed or without input.
* **Unexpected draw**: an outlet drawing power with no connection recorded.
* **Path incomplete**: a port connected to an outlet whose PDU has no feed.

Findings join the Inbox under a Power domain and follow the existing workflow.

== User interaction

* A site's power page lists panels and feeds with capacity bars for allocated
  and measured load.
* A rack shows its feeds, its PDUs' outlets, and which device each outlet
  serves, next to the elevation.
* A device's Hardware tab shows its power ports, their path to a feed, and a
  redundancy badge.
* "What fails if this feed fails" is a feed action listing every device that
  loses all of its supply, and every device that loses its redundancy.

Mutations require the managed-inventory owner/admin policy.

== Initial exclusions

UPS runtime modeling, generator and transfer-switch topology, cooling, power
billing, and automatic outlet control. Switching an outlet would be an `operate`
capability under RFD 9 and needs its own design.

== Rollout

. Add panels and feeds at sites and locations, with capacity.
. Create power ports and outlets from catalog templates on existing and new
  assets.
. Add power connections on the RFD 7 cable model and compute power paths.
. Add allocated load, redundancy, and the "what fails" view.
. Add power evidence and measured load from host agents and PDU collectors.
. Add the power findings and the Power Inbox domain.
